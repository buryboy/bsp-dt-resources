# Textiles — page briefs
Ids: 'p01'…'p11'. File names as in index.html. Times are guides (total ≈ 60 min).

- **p01-starter — 'Starter: the wardrobe challenge' (~4 min).** A pen-drawn wardrobe with three outfits for a day: a hot sweaty PE lesson, a freezing winter walk, a posh party. Unlabelled garments on hangers (they are cotton T-shirt, wool jumper, polyester sports top, silk shirt, nylon/elastane leggings, linen shirt…) each with a little "feel card" the student taps (described in words: soft, warm, shiny, stretchy…). Predict first ('p01.predict': does what a fabric is made of matter for how comfortable you feel?). Student drags garments onto a character for each scenario and presses GO: a comfort meter + thought bubbles (too hot / soaked with sweat / shivering / creased / perfect). Record 'p01.try'. Reveal after: fibres and fabrics are chosen for their properties; ui.mcq 'p01.check'; ui.rate 'p01.conf'. Fibre names only appear in the reveal.
- **p02-fibre-to-fabric — 'Fibre → yarn → fabric' (~6 min).** Interactive: magnifier over a jumper reveals the fabric, then the yarn, then fibres (zoom slider). A spinning wheel: student twists loose fibres (drag in a circle) and sees a yarn form and get stronger (pull test). Staple vs filament demo (short tufts vs a long silk filament unwinding from a cocoon). ui.sort/sequence 'p02.sequence' (mis fibre-yarn-fabric), ui.match 'p02.words' (fibre, yarn, fabric, spinning, staple, filament), ui.mcq on why twisting makes yarn stronger. Notes blurred during activities.
- **p03-natural — 'Natural fibres' (~7 min).** "Where does it come from?": four pen-drawn sources the student explores (tap the cotton boll to burst it open, pull flax stems and strip fibres, shear a sheep, unwind a cocoon). Then a fibre test bench (predict → test → conclude) with four swatches: warmth test (thermometer on a hand under each), absorbency (drip water: sinks in / beads), crease test (scrunch and release: wool springs back, linen stays creased), shine test (light reflection: silk). Records per test ('p03.warm', 'p03.absorb', 'p03.crease', 'p03.shine'; mis cotton-wool, natural-mixup, property-word). Notes after. ui.sort 'p03.family' (plant vs animal; mis plant-animal), ui.match 'p03.uses' (notes blurred; mis natural-mixup).
- **p04-synthetic — 'Synthetic fibres' (~6 min).** From oil drum to fibre: a pen spinneret animation (molten polymer squeezed through tiny holes into filaments). Three lab rigs: (1) stretch rig: hang weights on polyester, polyamide, elastane yarns and see stretch + spring back (elastane 5–7×); (2) quick-dry race: wet swatches of cotton vs polyester vs polyamide on a washing line with a clock slider; (3) the hot iron test: drag an iron over cotton vs polyester: polyester melts/glazes at high heat; heat-setting a pleat demo. Predict → test → conclude records ('p04.stretch', 'p04.dry', 'p04.iron'; mis synthetic-mixup, synthetic-melt). Notes after. ui.match 'p04.uses' (mis synthetic-mixup), ui.mcq 'p04.elastane' (why elastane is only a small % of leggings; mis elastane-alone), ui.tf on biodegradability/microfibres (mis synthetic-melt).
- **p05-blends — 'Blends & mixtures' (~5 min).** The blend mixer: a pen-drawn mixer with cotton and polyester hoppers and a % slider; live bars for comfort/absorbency, crease resistance, drying speed, strength, cost update only after the student presses SPIN (predict first what 65/35 polycotton will be like: 'p05.predict'). Challenges: make a school shirt that is comfy but needs no ironing (accept ~50–70% polyester); make stretch jeans (cotton + small % elastane: choose the right second fibre from a shelf). Records 'p05.c1', 'p05.c2' (mis polycotton-props, elastane-alone). Then ui.mcq 'p05.blend' (blend vs mixture; mis blend-meaning), ui.mcq 'p05.why' (why blend), notes blurred.
- **p06-weaving — 'The weaving loom' (~7 min).** A pen-drawn loom the student actually weaves on: warp threads strung vertically; the student taps a shed pattern / drags the weft shuttle across, choosing over/under; three patterns to weave (plain over-1-under-1; twill over-2-under-1 stepping each row → diagonal; satin floats over 4). Each completed swatch zooms out to show the real fabric (calico, denim, satin) and then a test: rub test (satin snags), look (twill diagonal). Records 'p06.plain', 'p06.twill', 'p06.satin' (max 1 each; mis weave-type). Then a cut-edge demo (frays) vs selvedge, and the warp/weft labelling activity: tap arrows / label on a drawn fabric piece ('p06.label': warp, weft, selvedge, bias; mis warp-weft, selvedge). ui.match 'p06.uses' (notes blurred).
- **p07-knit-nonwoven — 'Knitted & non-woven' (~6 min).** Knitting demo: a pen loop animation; student pulls a broken loop in weft knit and watches it ladder/unravel; does the same with warp knit (it holds). Stretch comparison: woven vs knitted swatch dragged sideways. Records 'p07.ladder' (predict which ladders first, then see; mis knit-type), 'p07.stretch' (mis woven-vs-knit). Felt workshop: student agitates wool fibres in a bowl with hot soapy water (rub/drag back and forth; a "heat, moisture, pressure, friction" checklist fills in) until they mat into felt; bonded fabric: a web of fibres + heat roller → interfacing / face mask. ui.mcq 'p07.felt' (mis nonwoven), ui.sort 'p07.sort' (10 products/properties into woven / weft knit / warp knit / felt / bonded; mis weave-type, knit-type, nonwoven), notes blurred.
- **p08-choose — 'Design the outfit' (~7 min).** Design briefs (6): e.g. a swimming costume, a winter coat for a hiker (outer + lining), a pair of jeans, a hospital disposable face mask, a summer shirt for a holiday in a hot country, a snooker table cover / a child's pyjamas (needs a flame retardant finish), school shirt that needs no ironing. Student picks fibre (and where relevant a construction: woven/knit/non-woven) and presses TEST: a pen scene shows a person using it and it succeeds or fails visibly (sweaty, sags, see-through when wet, shrinks, frays, too cold…). 'p08.b1'…'p08.b6' (max 1 each; all tries recorded; accept every sensible answer). ui.write 'p08.explain' [3 marks]: explain why a polycotton blend is used for school shirts.
- **p09-detective — 'Label detective' (~6 min).** 8 garment/product care-label case cards (the label gives fibre % and care symbols in words; drawing of the product) — which fibre/construction and why. ids 'p09.<case>-type' / '-why'. End ui.write 'p09.explain' [2 marks]: explain why elastane is added to swimwear.
- **p10-exam-practice — 'Exam practice' (~10 min, ≈13 marks).** AQA style; MCQ first; ui.write: name a natural fibre from an animal [1]; give two properties of wool that make it suitable for a jumper [2]; describe the difference between a blend and a mixture [2]; describe how a plain weave is constructed (sketch description) [2]; explain one advantage of a knitted fabric for sportswear [2]; explain one environmental issue with synthetic fibres [2]; state what a selvedge is [1] or similar.
- **p11-exit-quiz — 'Exit quiz' (~5 min).** 10 ui.mcq; ui.rate 'p11.conf-fibres', 'p11.conf-blends', 'p11.conf-construction'; ui.write 'p11.unsure' (marks 0).
- **final-quiz.js** — 20 questions {q,a,d,mis}, different from p11.
