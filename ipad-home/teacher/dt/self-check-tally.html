Self-check tally

Paste the revision lists your students emailed you from the Product Design self-check (or copied into Showbie). Paste as many as you like, one after another. It adds them up so you can see which topics need a re-teach or a new resource.

Everything stays in this browser tab: nothing is saved or sent, and refreshing the page clears it. If a student appears twice, only their last list is counted.
No sign-in and nothing is collected: these pages and files work like a book on a shelf. Workbook answers stay on your own iPad.
