How it works
  1. Rate every topic on pages 2 to 6.
  2. Tick Confident and an exam-style question appears. Answer it, then open the mark scheme and mark yourself honestly.
  3. Page 7 builds your revision list. Save it as a PDF for Showbie, and email the list to your teacher so they can make resources for the topics the class finds hardest.
The command word in each question is highlighted. Not sure what explain or discuss needs? See the Command words page in the Critical path workbook.
