Smart materials design challenge

GCSE · AQA 3.1.3 · About 30 minutes, plus a homework question

Students design something off the wall, such as a superhero costume, that uses at least two smart materials.

Smart materials design challengeOpen the student page

Link for students

https://dt.bsphosted.fr/smart-materials-challenge/ipad/

How it runs

  • 2 minutes: the brief, with stimulus and response explained.
  • 5 minutes: a six-question quick check. The material cards blur until it is finished.
  • 20 minutes: sketch the design on the page, then say where each smart material goes, what sets it off and what it does.
  • Homework: a 4-mark question on why a smart material beats a normal one.
  • Students save the page as a PDF and hand it in on Showbie. Nothing is sent from the page.

Year 7: today's making steps

Year 7 · practical lesson · On the board for the whole lesson

A board page that cycles through six cards: cut the base, smooth the edges, colour the backboard, cut extra pieces, plan the acrylic pieces, stay safe.

BoardOpen the board pageOpens full screen for the projector

How it runs

  • Put it on the board at the start of a practical lesson and leave it running.
  • It is a page for the board, not for students, so there is no student link.
  • It needs the internet for its fonts; without it the page still works in a plain font.
No sign-in and nothing is collected: these pages and files work like a book on a shelf. Workbook answers stay on your own iPad.
