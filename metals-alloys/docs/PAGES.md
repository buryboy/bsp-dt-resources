# Metals & alloys — page briefs
Ids: 'p01'…'p11'. File names as in index.html. Times are guides (total ≈ 60 min).

- **p01-starter — 'Starter: the scrapyard magnet' (~4 min).** A pen-drawn scrapyard crane with an electromagnet the student drags over a pile of 8 objects (steel car door, aluminium drinks can, copper pipe, cast-iron drain cover, brass tap, steel bolt, zinc-coated bucket (steel underneath: picked up), stainless-steel… leave stainless out here). Predict first (ui.predict 'p01.predict': how many of the 8 will the magnet lift? all / most / about half / none), then lift each one (some drop). Record 'p01.try'. After: reveal that the ones lifted all contain iron; ui.mcq 'p01.check'; ui.rate 'p01.conf'. Words ferrous/non-ferrous introduced only in the reveal.
- **p02-where-from — 'From rock to metal' (~6 min).** Ore → mine → extract → refine. Animated pen cutaway blast furnace the student "charges" (drag iron ore, coke, limestone in; tap to blast air; molten iron runs out, slag floats) and a simpler electrolysis cell for aluminium with an electricity meter racing. ui.sort or sequence 'p02.sequence' (put the stages in order), ui.match 'p02.ores' (iron ore/blast furnace, bauxite/electrolysis… mis ore-extraction), recycling: an interactive energy bar comparison (predict the saving first, 'p02.recycle'), ui.tf on finite/recycle (mis finite-recycle).
- **p03-ferrous — 'Ferrous metals & the carbon rule' (~7 min).** The carbon lab: a slider adds carbon to iron (0.1% → 3.5%); three test rigs respond live after the student predicts ('p03.predict'): a hardness dent test, a bend test (ductility: bends / snaps), and a drop/hit test (brittle cracking for cast iron). Student records conclusion 'p03.rule' (mis carbon-rule). Then the three ferrous metals appear on the carbon line with notes (after the lab). ui.sort 'p03.uses' (9–10 products into low carbon steel / high carbon steel / cast iron; mis steel-mixup; notes blurred), ui.fill 'p03.blanks'.
- **p04-non-ferrous — 'Non-ferrous metals' (~7 min).** Test bench for aluminium, copper, tin, zinc (and a steel block for comparison): (a) weigh same-size blocks on a balance (density; predict lightest first), (b) melt test with a thermometer (which melts first), (c) electrical conductivity meter, (d) leave outside in the rain for a year (time-lapse: steel rusts; others tarnish only; copper greens). Each test: predict → test → record ('p04.weigh', 'p04.melt', 'p04.conduct', 'p04.rain'). Notes on each metal and uses appear after. ui.match 'p04.uses' (8 products → metal; mis nonferrous-mixup, tin-can), notes blurred.
- **p05-alloys — 'Alloys: mixing metals' (~7 min).** What an alloy is and why (atoms-in-layers pen animation: pure metal layers slide; alloy atoms of a different size jam them; make it a small interactive: push the layers). The alloy kitchen: a crucible where the student chooses ingredients from a shelf (copper, zinc, tin, steel, chromium, nickel, tungsten, vanadium, carbon) to make a requested alloy: brass, stainless steel, high speed steel ('p05.brass', 'p05.stainless', 'p05.hss'; max 1 each; mis alloy-contents); the pour shows the result and what it is good for only after a correct mix; wrong mixes give an honest "that's not it" with a hint. ui.mcq 'p05.meaning' (mis alloy-meaning), ui.mcq 'p05.family' (stainless steel is a ferrous alloy; mis stainless-family), ui.match 'p05.uses' (notes blurred; mis alloy-mixup).
- **p06-rust-lab — 'The rust lab' (~6 min).** Classic three test tubes with a steel nail: (A) air + water, (B) boiled water with oil on top (water, no air), (C) dry air with drying agent (air, no water), plus (D) salt water + air. Predict which will rust first ('p06.predict'), then run a time-lapse (drag days 0–14); record 'p06.conclude' (mis rust-conditions). Then protection: a steel gate the student protects by choosing from paint / oil / galvanise / plastic coat / nothing, then scratches it and fast-forwards a year (painted+scratched rusts at the scratch; galvanised doesn't); ui.mcq 'p06.galv' (mis galvanise-meaning), ui.sort 'p06.rusts' (which metals rust: steel, cast iron, vs aluminium, copper, brass, stainless; mis ferrous-meaning).
- **p07-stock-and-id — 'Name that metal' (~5 min).** Identification game: 8 unlabelled pen-drawn sample bars; tools to test each: look (colour described in words + pen colour wash), magnet, weigh, scratch/file (hardness), spark? (no). Student identifies each from a list ('p07.id', max 8; mis steel-mixup, nonferrous-mixup, alloy-mixup). Then stock forms: ui.match 'p07.stock' (drawn cross-sections ↔ sheet, round bar, square bar, hexagonal bar, tube, angle).
- **p08-choose — 'Choose the metal' (~7 min).** Design briefs: 6 products (e.g. aircraft wing skin, kitchen sink, drill bit, wiring in a house, vice body, garden gate left outside, door handle/tap, food can). For each the student picks a metal from all ten and presses TEST: a pen scene shows it succeed or fail for a visible reason (too heavy, rusts, too soft, cracks, melts…). 'p08.b1'…'p08.b6' (max 1 each; all tries recorded; accept every sensible answer). Then ui.write 'p08.explain' [3 marks]: explain why stainless steel is used for a kitchen sink instead of low carbon steel.
- **p09-detective — 'Metal detective' (~6 min).** 8 case-file product cards (see levers-linkages p09 format): which metal + why (property). ids 'p09.<case>-metal' / '-why'. End ui.write 'p09.explain' [2 marks]: why are overhead power cables made from aluminium rather than copper? (lighter/low density so pylons carry less weight; still a good conductor; cheaper).
- **p10-exam-practice — 'Exam practice' (~10 min, ≈13 marks).** AQA style; MCQ first; ui.write with mark schemes/model answers: define ferrous [1]; name a non-ferrous metal and a use [2]; what is an alloy and why are they made [2]; explain why high carbon steel is used for a chisel [2]; describe how steel can be protected from rusting [2]; explain one environmental impact of extracting metals and one benefit of recycling [3 or 2+2]. Tip box example on something not asked.
- **p11-exit-quiz — 'Exit quiz' (~5 min).** 10 ui.mcq 'p11.q01'… ; ui.rate 'p11.conf-ferrous', 'p11.conf-nonferrous', 'p11.conf-alloys'; ui.write 'p11.unsure' (marks 0).
