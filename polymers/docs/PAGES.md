# Polymers — page briefs
Ids: 'p01'…'p11'. File names as in index.html. Times are guides (total ≈ 60 min).

- **p01-starter — 'Starter: the hot pan test' (~4 min).** A pen-drawn hot frying pan. Two near-identical black handles/spoons: a nylon/PP-looking spatula and a saucepan handle. Predict first (ui.predict 'p01.predict': what happens to each when left on the hot pan?), then drag each onto the pan and hold: one sags and droops (and when cooled stays in the new shape; reheat and reshape it again), the other stays rigid then eventually chars but never softens. Record 'p01.try'. Reveal after: two families exist (names introduced only here); ui.mcq 'p01.check' (mis thermo-swap); ui.rate 'p01.conf'.
- **p02-oil-to-polymer — 'From oil to polymer' (~6 min).** Interactive pen-drawn journey: oil rig → fractionating column (tap to heat; fractions rise and separate; naphtha highlighted) → cracker (tap to crack long molecules into monomers) → polymerisation: a chain builder where the student drags/taps monomers together to make a long chain (counter of units; "poly = many"). ui.sort/sequence 'p02.sequence' (order the stages; mis polymer-source), ui.mcq 'p02.mono' (mis monomer-polymer), additives: ui.match 'p02.additives' (pigment/plasticiser/stabiliser ↔ job; mis additive-mixup), ui.tf on finite oil (mis oil-renewable). Notes blurred during activities.
- **p03-two-families — 'Thermoforming vs thermosetting' (~7 min).** The chain model lab: two canvases of pen-drawn tangled chains, one without cross-links, one with. A heat slider: the student heats each (predict first 'p03.predict'): thermoforming chains wriggle and slide apart, a test strip above bends and can be re-formed over and over (count the re-shapes); thermoset chains stay locked, strip stays rigid then chars. Conclude 'p03.conclude' (ui.mcq; mis crosslink). Plastic memory demo: a vacuum-formed HIPS shape reheated returns to a flat sheet ('p03.memory' predict then see). Notes after. ui.sort 'p03.sort' (10 statements into thermoforming / thermosetting; mis thermo-swap, thermoset-recycle; notes blurred). ui.fill 'p03.blanks'.
- **p04-thermoforming — 'The thermoforming six' (~8 min).** Test bench with six unlabelled-property sample sheets named only by polymer: tests the student runs on each: drop a ball/hammer (toughness: acrylic cracks, HIPS/PP/HDPE survive), bend back and forth 20 times (PP hinge survives; acrylic snaps; HIPS whitens/cracks), scratch test (acrylic scratches; hard though), see-through test (acrylic & PET clear), chemical splash (HDPE/PP unaffected). Results table fills in as they test; record 'p04.tests' (which tests done) and a conclusion question per key idea: 'p04.acrylic' (mis acrylic-tough), 'p04.hinge' (mis pp-hinge), 'p04.clear'. Notes on all six appear after. ui.match 'p04.uses' (8–10 products → polymer; mis thermoform-mixup; notes blurred).
- **p05-thermosetting — 'The thermosetting five' (~6 min).** A two-part mixing demo: mix resin + hardener (epoxy) and watch it set; try to reheat it. Then five product "exhibits" each demonstrating why a thermoset is needed (saucepan handle on a hot hob, plug carrying electricity, picnic plate scratched/hot food, boat hull with glass fibre layers the student lays up and tests vs resin alone, circuit board). Student matches polymer to exhibit after exploring ('p05.exhibits', max 5; mis thermoset-mixup), ui.mcq 'p05.grp' (what GRP is / why add glass fibre), ui.mcq 'p05.why' (why not a thermoforming polymer for a pan handle; mis thermo-swap), ui.fill 'p05.names' (abbreviations ER, MF, PF, PR, UF ↔ names as a match). Notes blurred.
- **p06-id-lab — 'Name that plastic' (~6 min).** Identification game: 8 unlabelled everyday items (milk bottle, fizzy bottle, yoghurt pot, lunch box lid with hinge, drain pipe, shop sign, plug, worktop sample). Tools: look/feel card, flex test, heat test (softens or not), float test in water (HDPE & PP float; PET, PVC, acrylic sink — say "floats: less dense than water"), check for recycling code stamp (thermoforming only). Student identifies each ('p06.id', max 8; mis thermoform-mixup, thermoset-mixup, thermo-swap).
- **p07-recycling — 'Polymers & the planet' (~6 min).** A recycling-centre conveyor game: items roll past with their code number; the student flicks each into the right bin (1 PET, 2 HDPE, 3 PVC, 5 PP, 6 PS, "cannot be remelted": thermosets) with a score ('p07.sort', mis code-mixup, thermoset-recycle). Then a time-lapse landfill/ocean slider (apple core, paper, steel can, plastic bottle: how long each lasts; predict first 'p07.predict'). ui.sort 'p07.6r' or ui.mcq set on reduce/reuse/recycle/bio-polymers ('p07.actions'), ui.tf on microplastics/biodegradable (mis oil-renewable).
- **p08-choose — 'Choose the polymer' (~7 min).** Design briefs: 6 products (fizzy drink bottle, electric kettle body? (PP), light switch, vacuum-formed chocolate tray, outdoor shop sign, canoe/boat hull, flip-top bottle cap, pan handle). Student picks from all eleven polymers and presses TEST: pen scene shows success or a visible failure (melts, shatters, snaps at hinge, goes cloudy, not food safe…). 'p08.b1'…'p08.b6' (max 1 each; all tries recorded; accept every sensible answer). ui.write 'p08.explain' [3 marks]: explain why polypropylene is a good choice for a lunch box with a hinged lid.
- **p09-detective — 'Polymer detective' (~6 min).** 8 case-file cards (levers-linkages p09 format): which polymer + why. End ui.write 'p09.explain' [2 marks]: explain why electrical plugs are made from urea formaldehyde.
- **p10-exam-practice — 'Exam practice' (~10 min, ≈13 marks).** AQA style; MCQ first; ui.write: state the raw material most polymers come from [1]; describe the difference between thermoforming and thermosetting polymers [2]; name a thermoforming polymer suitable for X and give a reason [2]; explain why thermosetting polymers cannot be recycled by melting [2]; explain two environmental problems caused by polymers [4 or 2+2]; give two properties that make acrylic suitable for a shop sign [2].
- **p11-exit-quiz — 'Exit quiz' (~5 min).** 10 ui.mcq; ui.rate 'p11.conf-families', 'p11.conf-thermoforming', 'p11.conf-thermosetting'; ui.write 'p11.unsure' (marks 0).
