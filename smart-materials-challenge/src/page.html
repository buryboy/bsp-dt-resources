Smart materials design challenge

GCSE Design & Technology · AQA 3.1.3 · one lesson

Design something only smart materials could make

1The brief 2 minutes

A smart material changes one of its properties when something around it changes, and changes back again afterwards. The thing that sets it off is the stimulus. What the material does is the response.

Your job: design something a bit off the wall that uses at least two smart materials. A superhero costume, a pet gadget, kit for an extreme sport, anything you like. Every smart material must do a real job in your design.

A good design shows:

  • a clear, labelled sketch
  • two or more smart materials, named correctly
  • the stimulus and the response for each one
  • why a normal material could not do that job
  • one problem with your design and how you would solve it

2Smart material cards

Tap a card to turn it over. Green cards are named on the AQA specification, so learn those three. The others are extra ideas for your design.

The cards are hidden until you finish the quick check.

3Quick check 5 minutes

Choose the smart material that fits each description. Once you start, the cards are hidden until you finish.

4Your design 20 minutes

Pick a starting point, or use your own idea.

Photograph your drawing only, with no people in the picture. You can draw labels on top of it.
Smart materialWhere is it?Stimulus: what sets it off?Response: what does it do, and why is that useful?

5Finish for homework exam practice

Explain why one of the smart materials in your design is a better choice than a normal material for that job. [4 marks]

Tip: name the material, say what the stimulus is, say how it responds, then say what a normal material would do instead.
