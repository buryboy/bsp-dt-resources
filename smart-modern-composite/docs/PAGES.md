# Smart, modern & composite materials — page briefs
Ids: 'p01'…'p11'. File names as in index.html. Times are guides (total ≈ 60 min).

- **p01-starter — 'Starter: the mystery objects' (~4 min).** A pen-drawn "museum of the future" table with 5 unlabelled objects: a mug (thermochromic), a pair of glasses (photochromic lenses), a bent paperclip-like wire (SMA), a racing bike frame (CFRP), a firefighter glove (fire resistant). Predict first ('p01.predict': can a material change itself without electricity?). Student pokes each one: pour hot tea (mug changes colour), take glasses outside (sun slider: lenses darken), bend the wire then dip in hot water (it springs back), lift the bike (weigh: tiny), hold glove in flame (no burn). Record 'p01.try'. Reveal after: four groups exist (names appear only in the reveal); ui.mcq 'p01.check' (mis smart-meaning); ui.rate 'p01.conf'.
- **p02-modern — 'Modern materials' (~7 min).** Three benches, predict → test → conclude: (1) graphene: zoom into a pencil line (graphite) and peel off a single layer of carbon atoms (hexagons, pen-drawn), then a strength-for-weight test (a graphene sheet one atom thick holding a weight vs foil; illustrative) and a conductivity circuit; (2) metal foam: drop a heavy weight onto a solid aluminium block vs aluminium foam vs nothing; foam crumples and absorbs, plus a float test; (3) titanium: a corrosion tank (salt water, years slider) with steel vs titanium + a weighing scale vs steel + a body-implant "rejection" check. Records 'p02.graphene', 'p02.foam', 'p02.titanium' (mis graphene-structure, metal-foam, titanium-props). Notes after each. ui.match 'p02.uses' (notes blurred; mis modern-mixup).
- **p03-smart-pigments — 'Smart pigments' (~6 min).** Two colour-changing labs. Thermochromic: a mug + kettle + temperature slider; a baby spoon in soup; a battery tester strip the student presses; record when the colour flips. Photochromic: glasses lenses/T-shirt under a sun/cloud/indoors slider (and a UV torch). Predict-first for each ('p03.thermo-predict', 'p03.photo-predict'); conclusions 'p03.thermo', 'p03.photo' (mis stimulus-mixup). Then "is it smart?": 6 quick cases (mood ring, colour-changing LED light, chocolate melting, photochromic nail varnish, traffic light, kettle that changes colour) → smart / not smart ('p03.smart', mis smart-meaning). ui.match 'p03.uses' (notes blurred).
- **p04-sma — 'Shape memory alloys' (~6 min).** The nitinol lab: a wire the student bends by dragging into any shape (squiggle), then heats (drag a hairdryer/ dip in hot water) and watches it spring back to its programmed straight/remembered shape (animated, pen-drawn); then "program" it: hold in a new shape and heat very hot, cool, bend, reheat → it returns to the new shape. Superelastic glasses frame: sit on them (drag a chair) → spring back. Actuator: an SMA wire lifting a weight when a small current heats it (then cools and stretches). Records 'p04.recover' (predict then see), 'p04.program', 'p04.actuator' (mis sma-mixup). Notes after. ui.match 'p04.uses' (braces, stents, glasses, sprinkler, anti-scald valve) notes blurred; ui.tf (needs a battery? false; mis smart-meaning).
- **p05-composites — 'Composites' (~7 min).** What a composite is: the mud-and-straw brick demo (pull test: mud alone cracks, with straw holds) to introduce matrix + reinforcement. Concrete beam lab: a beam on two supports; student loads the top (compression fine) and adds weights until it cracks from the bottom (tension); then adds steel rebar near the bottom (drag bars in) and retests; predict where it will crack first ('p05.beam-predict'); record 'p05.beam' (mis concrete-tension). GRP vs CFRP lay-up: student lays up a canoe/bike frame from a shelf (resin type + fibre) then tests strength/weight/cost; records 'p05.layup' (mis gfrp-cfrp, matrix-reinforcement). Notes after. ui.sort 'p05.parts' (cement, aggregate, steel rebar, polyester resin, glass fibre, epoxy resin, carbon fibre → matrix / reinforcement; mis matrix-reinforcement), ui.mcq 'p05.alloy' (composite vs alloy; mis composite-meaning).
- **p06-tech-textiles — 'Technical textiles' (~6 min).** Four test stations: (1) touchscreen glove: tap a phone with a normal glove vs a conductive-thread glove; light-up circuit through conductive thread; (2) flame test: hold swatches (cotton, polyester, fire-resistant aramid) to a flame: cotton burns, polyester melts and drips, FR self-extinguishes; (3) Kevlar: a knife cut test and a pull test vs nylon vs steel wire by weight; (4) micro-encapsulation: zoom into a microfibre with capsules; rub the fabric (friction) and capsules burst releasing scent puffs, count down over "washes". Predict-first and record per station ('p06.conductive', 'p06.flame', 'p06.kevlar', 'p06.capsules'; mis tech-textile-mixup, kevlar-props, microencap). Notes after. ui.match 'p06.uses' (notes blurred).
- **p07-sort-it — 'Which group?' (~4 min).** A fast sorting game: 16 material/product cards fly in; student flicks/taps each into modern / smart / composite / technical textile (e.g. graphene, nitinol, CFRP, Kevlar, thermochromic pigment, metal foam, concrete, conductive thread, titanium, photochromic lens, GRP, microencapsulated sock, fire resistant suit, reinforced concrete, plywood, Nomex). Record 'p07.sort' (max 16; mis group-mixup). Then ui.mcq 'p07.define' on the four definitions (one mcq each or a match 'p07.define').
- **p08-choose — 'Choose the material' (~7 min).** Design briefs (6): e.g. a baby feeding spoon that warns if food is too hot; a Formula 1 car body; a hip replacement; sunglasses that work indoors and out; a firefighter's jacket; a bridge deck; a car crumple zone; a police vest. Student picks from all materials in CONTENT and presses TEST: pen scene shows success or visible failure. 'p08.b1'…'p08.b6' (max 1 each; all tries recorded; accept every sensible answer). ui.write 'p08.explain' [3 marks]: explain why CFRP is used for racing bike frames even though it is expensive.
- **p09-detective — 'Material detective' (~6 min).** 8 case-file product cards (levers-linkages p09 format): which material + why. End ui.write 'p09.explain' [2 marks]: explain how a shape memory alloy is used in dental braces.
- **p10-exam-practice — 'Exam practice' (~10 min, ≈13 marks).** AQA style; MCQ first; ui.write: define a smart material [2]; give one use of thermochromic pigment and explain how it works [2]; explain why steel rods are put in concrete [2]; name the two parts of a composite and what each does [2]; give two properties of titanium that make it suitable for a hip replacement [2]; describe how micro-encapsulation works in a fabric [2]; state one use of Kevlar [1].
- **p11-exit-quiz — 'Exit quiz' (~5 min).** 10 ui.mcq; ui.rate 'p11.conf-modern', 'p11.conf-smart', 'p11.conf-composite', 'p11.conf-textiles'; ui.write 'p11.unsure' (marks 0).
