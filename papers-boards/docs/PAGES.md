# Papers & boards — page briefs
Ids: 'p01'…'p11'. File names as in index.html. Times are guides (total ≈ 60 min).

- **p01-starter — 'Starter: the marker test' (~4 min).** Four unlabelled pen-drawn sheets (A–D) on a desk. Predict first (ui.predict 'p01.predict': draw a line with a marker on each: will they all look the same?). Then the student draws on each sheet with a finger using a marker tool: on one the ink feathers/spreads and soaks through (ordinary thin paper), on one it stays crisp (bleed proof), on one it shows through to a drawing underneath (layout), one is rough/thick (cartridge). Flip each sheet to see the back. Record 'p01.try'. Reveal after: papers are engineered for jobs (names later); ui.mcq 'p01.check'; ui.rate 'p01.conf'.
- **p02-tree-to-paper — 'From tree to paper' (~6 min).** Interactive pen-drawn paper mill the student operates stage by stage (tap/drag: fell, debark, chip, pulp with water, bleach, spray on wire mesh, press, dry, calender, reel). ui.sort/sequence 'p02.sequence' (order 6–7 stages; mis paper-source), recycling loop interactive: recycle a sheet again and again and watch fibres under a magnifier get shorter until it fails (predict how many times first: 'p02.recycle-predict'; then 'p02.recycle' mcq; mis recycle-forever), ui.match 'p02.words' (cellulose, pulp, FSC, calendering…), ui.tf renewable/biodegradable (mis renewable-mix). Notes blurred during activities.
- **p03-gsm-sizes — 'gsm, microns & A sizes' (~7 min).** (a) gsm: a balance where the student places 1 m² sheets of different papers (predict heaviest), notes after; (b) the A-size folding interactive: fold/cut an A0 in half repeatedly, sizes appear as they go; (c) microns with a pen-drawn micrometer the student closes on different boards. Calculations with ui.fill echoing the student's own earlier answers: 'p03.calc1' (weight of one A4 sheet of 80 gsm → uses 1/16), 'p03.calc2' (a ream of 500 sheets of a different gsm), 'p03.calc3' (microns ↔ mm: e.g. 750 microns = ? mm; 1.5 mm = ? microns), 'p03.calc4' (A-size dimensions: A3 from A4, A5 from A4). Use numbers different from CONTENT's worked examples. ui.mcq 'p03.gsm' (mis gsm-meaning), ui.mcq 'p03.asize' (mis a-size).
- **p04-papers — 'The five papers' (~7 min).** Test desk: five sheets named by type; tests: marker test (bleed), see-through test over a drawing (layout translucent, tracing clear, cartridge opaque), pencil shading/texture test (cartridge grips), weigh (gsm), grid reveal. Results table fills as they test; conclusion questions 'p04.marker' (mis bleed-meaning), 'p04.trace' (mis layout-vs-tracing), 'p04.sketch'. Notes after. ui.match 'p04.uses' (7–8 design-studio jobs → paper; mis paper-mixup; notes blurred).
- **p05-design-studio — 'A day in the design studio' (~5 min).** A short story-driven sequence: follow a designer developing a product from first sketches to final presentation board; at each of 6 steps the student hands them the right paper (tap choice; 'p05.steps', max 6; mis paper-mixup, layout-vs-tracing, bleed-meaning), with the designer's pen drawing appearing on the chosen paper (wrong choices visibly go wrong: ink bleeds, can't see through). ui.write 'p05.explain' [2 marks]: explain why a designer uses layout paper when developing ideas.
- **p06-boards — 'The six boards' (~7 min).** Cross-section explorer: six pen-drawn board samples the student "peels"/zooms to reveal the layers (flutes and liners; grey back and white coated face; foil layer; foam core; coated surface; white all through). For each, after exploring, ui.mcq on its structure or key property ('p06.corrugated' mis corrugated-structure, 'p06.duplex' mis duplex-vs-solid, 'p06.foil' mis foil-purpose, 'p06.foam', 'p06.inkjet', 'p06.solid'); notes after each. ui.match 'p06.uses' (8 products → board; mis board-mixup; notes blurred).
- **p07-board-lab — 'The packaging test lab' (~6 min).** Three rigs, predict then test: (1) drop test: an egg/phone packed in each board type dropped from rising heights (corrugated protects best), (2) keep-it-hot: a hot takeaway meal under different lids with a thermometer over 20 minutes (foil lined keeps heat, stops soggy), (3) weight-for-stiffness: bridge a gap with each board and load it / weigh it (foam core: stiff and very light; corrugated direction of flutes matters as a bonus). Records 'p07.drop', 'p07.hot', 'p07.stiff' (+ predicts). ui.mcq 'p07.laminate' (why laminate card with polythene/foil for a juice carton; mis foil-purpose).
- **p08-choose — 'Choose the paper or board' (~7 min).** Packaging designer briefs: 6 jobs (box to post a mug, cereal box, perfume box printed inside and out, takeaway lid, architect's model walls, tracing a logo, marker rendering, printing a photo). Student picks from all eleven and presses TEST: pen scene shows success or visible failure. 'p08.b1'…'p08.b6' (max 1 each; all tries recorded; accept every sensible answer). ui.write 'p08.explain' [3 marks]: explain why corrugated card is used for boxes that are sent through the post.
- **p09-detective — 'Paper detective' (~6 min).** 8 case-file cards (levers-linkages p09 format): which paper/board + why. End ui.write 'p09.explain' [2 marks]: explain why a cereal box is made from duplex board rather than solid white board.
- **p10-exam-practice — 'Exam practice' (~10 min, ≈13 marks).** AQA style; MCQ first; ui.write: state what gsm stands for [1]; name a paper for marker rendering and give a reason [2]; describe the structure of corrugated card [2]; calculation (weight of sheets or microns) [2]; explain why foil lined board is used for takeaway lids [2]; explain one advantage and one disadvantage of using recycled paper [2+2 or 3].
- **p11-exit-quiz — 'Exit quiz' (~5 min).** 10 ui.mcq; ui.rate 'p11.conf-papers', 'p11.conf-boards', 'p11.conf-sizes'; ui.write 'p11.unsure' (marks 0).
- **final-quiz.js** — 20 questions {q,a,d,mis}, different from p11.
